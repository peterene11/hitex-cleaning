Questions

Do I need to buy cleaning products?

No. The crew brings everything. If you'd like a particular product used, leave it out and tell the crew lead.

Do I need to be at home?

Someone needs to let the crew in and be there at the end for the walk-through. You don't need to stay for the whole clean.

How long does it take?

A regular clean of a 3-bedroom flat takes 3 to 4 hours with two cleaners. Deep cleans and new buildings can take a full day. You get an estimate when Hitex confirms.

What if there's no light?

Most of the work is done by hand. For vacuuming and scrubbing machines the crew needs power, from the grid or your generator or inverter. Let Hitex know when you book.

Can I change the date?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before the visit. Call or message either Hitex line.
